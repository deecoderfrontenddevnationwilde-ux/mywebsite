Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dee Coder Technologies (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit this Website, contact us, subscribe to updates, or view advertising if it is enabled here. It also explains how Google advertising services, including Google Ad Manager, and other advertising partners may process information if configured on the Website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Website, we may collect:

  • Information you provide, such as your name, email address, subject, and message when you use the contact form, and your email address and subscription status when you sign up for the newsletter.
  • Technical and usage information, which may include your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and browser information, pages viewed, referring page, and interaction or diagnostic data recorded by the Website or its service providers.
  • Information collected through cookies or similar technologies by us or by third parties, including advertising services, as described below.

2. How Information Is Collected

We collect information when you submit a form or subscribe. Some technical data may be recorded automatically by the Website's hosting, security, or operational systems. If advertising services are enabled on a page, Google and other advertising partners may collect information through cookies, device identifiers, tags, or similar technologies.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to respond to enquiries, administer newsletter subscriptions and opt-outs, operate and protect the Website, diagnose technical issues, and understand or improve how the Website is used. Information may also be processed to select, deliver, measure, and limit advertising, in accordance with the settings and services in use.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies can store or access information in your browser or device. They may support essential site functions, remember choices, measure site use, or deliver and measure advertisements. Some may be set by third parties rather than by us. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking or deleting them may affect some website or advertising functionality.

5. Advertising Cookies and Ad Choices

Advertising may be contextual (based on the page or general context) or personalized (selected using information associated with your interests, activity, or browser/device), depending on available settings, applicable requirements, and the advertising services enabled. Non-personalized advertising is not selected based on a personalized profile, but its delivery or measurement may still involve limited technical data, cookies, or similar technologies.

Google and other partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and protect advertising. The information used and available choices can depend on your location, account status, browser, device, and the settings configured for the ad service. Use your browser controls and Google's advertising settings or privacy controls, where available, to review or change choices. Your choice with one provider may not control other advertising partners.

6. Google Ad Manager and Google Advertising Services

The Website may use Google advertising services, including Google Ad Manager, to serve or manage advertisements. If enabled, Google may receive or collect information such as IP address, cookie or device identifiers, browser and device details, ad requests and impressions, and interactions with ads. Google may use this information to provide, maintain, measure, and protect its services and, where enabled and permitted, to personalize advertising.

Google processes information under its own terms and privacy disclosures. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and review Google's information about advertising technologies. The exact processing depends on the services and settings active when you visit.

7. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Google Ad Manager may support advertising from Google and, depending on configuration, other advertising partners. Those partners may receive information or use their own cookies and similar technologies to serve, measure, or protect ads. Their collection and use are governed by their respective privacy notices. Partner participation and available controls may vary over time and by location.

8. Analytics Services

If analytics services are enabled on the Website, they may collect usage and device information, such as pages visited, interactions, browser details, and approximate location, to help us understand site performance and usage. The analytics provider and its privacy terms will apply to its processing. This policy will be updated if our analytics use materially changes.

9. How Information Is Shared

We may share information with service providers that help operate the Website, store or deliver messages, or provide advertising and measurement services, including Google and configured advertising partners. We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to protect the Website or our rights, comply with a valid legal process, or address security or safety concerns. We do not control how independent third parties use information they collect directly through their services.

10. Data Retention and Security

We retain contact messages and subscription records for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry or subscription and for related operational, security, and recordkeeping needs. If you unsubscribe, we may retain a limited record of that choice to honor it. Retention by Google, advertising partners, hosting providers, or other service providers is governed by their practices. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of, or limits on certain personal information, or to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your request, and some information may be retained where permitted or required. Choices for information held by Google or another partner should be directed to that provider as well.

12. Managing Cookies and Advertising Preferences

You can adjust cookie handling in your browser and review Google's ad personalization settings and Google's advertising privacy information. You may also be able to manage certain ad choices through your device or regional preference tools. These controls are provider-specific, may not disable all advertising, and may need to be set separately on each browser or device. Where the Website provides a consent or preference prompt, you can use it to manage the choices it covers.

13. Children's Privacy

The Website is not intended to solicit personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the contact or newsletter forms. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review and take appropriate steps.

14. International Data Transfers

We are based in Lagos, Nigeria. Our service providers, including Google and advertising partners, may process information in countries other than the one where you are located. Where applicable, those transfers and processing are subject to the providers' safeguards and the requirements that apply to the relevant service and location.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as the Website, advertising configuration, or our practices change. We will post the revised policy here and update the “Last Updated” date. Please review this page periodically.

16. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Dee Coder Technologies at deecodertechnologiesworldwide@gmail.com, use the website contact form, or message us on WhatsApp at +234 807 534 6224. Our published location is Lagos, Nigeria.
